About these results

Red-flag Research is a research project that screens conference submissions automatically. A Source review recommended label means the system found passages, numbers or tables that closely match an earlier public paper without a clear attribution, so a person should compare the two papers side by side.

It is not a finding of plagiarism, misconduct or bad faith. It has not been reviewed by the conference, the authors or any integrity committee. The system uses language models and can be wrong: shared authors, an earlier version of the same work, a cited dataset or a common baseline can all explain matching text, and the system may not see them. A paper without a label has not been cleared either.

Benchmark cases are anonymized before release.

Think a result is wrong?

If you are an author and believe a result about your paper is wrong, use the feedback form or email red.flag.autoresearch@gmail.com. We will correct it right away, and we take the result off the live page and the public data while we check.
